# Code of Conduct

We want func to be a friendly place to learn and build, for people of every experience level.

**Do**
- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give feedback on the work, and be specific and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

**Do not**
- Harass, insult or demean anyone.
- Post other people's private information.
- Keep going after someone asks you to stop.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

If someone breaks these rules, maintainers may edit or remove their contributions, or block them from the project. To report a problem, open a private security advisory titled "Conduct" at https://github.com/parsecraft/func/security/advisories/new, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile at https://github.com/parsecraft.
